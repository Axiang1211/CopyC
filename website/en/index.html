FAQ

Ask the uncle before you order.

Does CopyC send what I copy to the internet?

No. What you copy is only used to draw the pill on your own computer, and recent items live in memory only — they’re gone when you quit. The only thing CopyC asks the internet is GitHub’s latest version number, which carries none of your content and can be turned off in settings.

Does it work by watching my keyboard?

No. CopyC listens for the system’s clipboard-change events and never records keystrokes. That’s why it works with shortcuts, right-click menus and “copy” buttons on web pages — and why pressing Ctrl+C with nothing selected doesn’t set it off.

Why do I see a security warning when installing?

The installers aren’t signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper warn you. Follow the “Install tips” under each download card. On Windows you can also install from the Microsoft Store instead: no warning, and updates arrive automatically.

Why “CopyC”?

“Copy” sounds almost exactly like “Kopi”, Malay for coffee — and Kopi C is the Nanyang coffee-shop classic with evaporated milk. Copying something should feel like ordering your usual Kopi.
