Delete your account and data

Delete all CopyC data, any time.

CopyC (developer: DreamsWorld) has no accounts of its own — sync uses your Google account. Our servers don’t store what you copy or your email, so your data lives only on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. Follow the steps below to delete all of it; there’s nothing to request from us.

1. Delete the backup and encryption key in Google Drive

  • On any computer, open CopyC → Settings → Sync → Delete cloud data. All encrypted history and the key that CopyC keeps in your Google Drive app folder are deleted right away.
  • Without a computer: open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → CopyC → Options → Delete hidden app data.

2. Delete data on your phone (Android)

  • In CopyC’s Settings tab, tap Sign out: access is revoked and the sign-in token and history on the phone are deleted.
  • Or uninstall CopyC — all of its data on the phone is deleted with it.

3. Delete data on your computers (Windows, macOS)

  • CopyC → Settings → History → Clear; then Sign out under Sync and uninstall.

4. Remove CopyC’s access to your Google account

  • Open Google Account → Third-party apps & services → CopyC → Delete all connections.

What we keep, and for how long

  • What you copy: our relay only passes along end-to-end encrypted content. It stores nothing and can’t decrypt it.
  • Email and Google sign-in token: used only to check which account you are; never stored.
  • Google Drive backup: kept in your own Google Drive and deleted automatically by CopyC after 90 days, or right away with step 1.
